Understand the basics

What is a Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases — mainly carbon dioxide (CO₂) — produced directly or indirectly by our everyday actions. Every time we travel, switch on a light, eat a meal, or use a plastic bag, we add a little more carbon to the atmosphere. Understanding this number helps us make smarter, greener choices that protect our planet for future generations.

Transportation

Fuel-powered vehicles release CO₂ with every kilometer travelled.

Electricity

Most power grids still rely on fossil fuels to generate electricity.

Food

Meat production generates far more emissions than plant-based food.

Plastic Waste

Plastic production and disposal both release harmful greenhouse gases.
